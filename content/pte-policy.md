**Important Information: PTE Requests and Course Enrollment**

Dear Student,

If you're considering sending an email to request a PTE, please note the following points:

1. The department does not permit the issuance of PTEs.
2. Course enrollment is initially open to Statistics majors, and later becomes available to all students. You should be able to enroll once it opens to everyone.
3. If the class is full or you're on the waitlist and unable to enroll, please contact the department's staff, for example Jose or Stephanie, for assistance with enrollment issues. You can find their contact information [here](https://statistics.ucla.edu/index.php/people1/staff/career-staff/).

Regardless of your enrollment status, you are always welcome to attend the class.
