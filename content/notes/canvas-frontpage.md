---
title: 'How to have a frontpage in Canvas?'
date: 2023-01-11
---
Assume that you have a course in Canvas (a.k.a Bruin Learn if you are at UCLA), and you want to have a frontpage display on the home page with information pointing to the course website, etc. You can following the steps below:

1. Go to the [Bruin Learn](https://bruinlearn.ucla.edu/) website.
2. Select your course, etc.
3. Select **Pages** from the left panel: ![](/assets/img/canvas-pages.png)
4. You most likely have to click on the **View All Pages** button at the top: ![](/assets/img/canvas-view-all-pages.png)
5. Create your front page by clicking on the **+Page**: ![](/assets/img/canvas-ppage.png)
6. Save and Publish the page.
7. In the list of pages, click on ⋮ for you page and choose "Use as Front Page".
8. From the left panel, choose **Home** and from the right panel there select **Choose Home Page**. Then, choose the page you designed as your front page: ![](/assets/img/canvas-right-panel.png)

Done!
