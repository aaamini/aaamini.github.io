## Textbook(s)
There is no official textbook for this course. I will provide lecture notes and slides. But here is a list of possible textbooks you can chose from:

- [An Introduction to Statistical Learning](https://www.statlearning.com/), James, Witten, Hastie, Tibshirani, 2nd Ed.
- [The Elements of Statistical Learning](https://hastie.su.domains/ElemStatLearn/), Hastie, Tibshirani, Friedman, 2nd Ed.
- [Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/uploads/prod/2006/01/Bishop-Pattern-Recognition-and-Machine-Learning-2006.pdf), Bishop.

- [Applied Predictive Modeling](http://appliedpredictivemodeling.com/toc)
- [A Course in Machine Learning](http://ciml.info/) Daumé III.
